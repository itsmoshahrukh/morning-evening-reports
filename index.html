Excel reports

The standard report formats: Blue Wall Morning and Evening shifts, Harvest weekly, and Sales & Margin for any “with Cost” export.

  1. Exports to report on. Morning and Evening together is fine; they become separate reports. A station-only export (like Yum Bakery) is merged into its shift.
  2. Comparison exports (optional). The previous trading day or the same day last week. Each shift is matched to its shift.
  3. Build, then download. Every report is reconciled to the register before it is built.
